Email us for help

Or email us directly at andmore.games.help@gmail.com. The button opens your mail app with the subject already filled in.

When you write, tell us: your device (iPhone / iPad), iOS version, which day it happened on, and what you saw. A screenshot helps.

Common answers

  • Progress is saved on your device, per level. Deleting the app deletes your saved coins, colours, upgrades and unlocked days.
  • Picks are final once a rider is boarded — tap carefully.
  • Ads appear before each level, never in the middle of a day.
  • Pack the Lift is free. There are no in-app purchases.
